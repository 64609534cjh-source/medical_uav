We changed the reward to +1 for an on-time delivery, +0.1 for a late one, −0.1 when an order expires and −0.001 per battery point. Under the M1 reward a late delivery still earned up to +1 and an expiry cost 0.5, so the reward favoured rescuing late orders: a visible-order rollout planner scored with it served 96.6% of the orders at medium load but only 83.1% on time, while with the new reward the same planner reaches 89.7% on time and still serves 94.6%. The global observation now also contains the 7 × 7 matrix of travel times between all nodes (49 features), the "route distances and travel times" of our proposal. We added the proposal's FCFS and nearest-first rules, and `scripts/evaluate.py` runs any policies and trained networks on the same days and reports paired bootstrap intervals. Development, model selection and the final test use separate sets of days.
