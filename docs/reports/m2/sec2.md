`bc.py` and `scripts/train_bc.py` train both networks to imitate Slack-aware, our strongest rule (behaviour cloning). They record its decisions, with the observation and mask a network would see, on 900 days (demand seeds 0–299 at each of the three loads; 88,744 decisions), kept apart from the days we evaluate on. The days are split 80/20 by demand seed, so validation days are never seen in training. Training minimises the cross-entropy between the masked logits and the teacher's action (Adam, learning rate 3·10⁻⁴, batch 256, 30 epochs, keeping the epoch with the lowest validation loss) and takes about a minute on a CPU. The value head is not trained here; PPO will train it.

Despite validation accuracies of 93.5% (MLP) and 93.9% (set), their mean on-time rates over whole days are 1.5–3.2 points below the teacher at medium and high load (Table 1; for the set network the paired 95% intervals still include zero). This is consistent with compounding imitation errors, a known weakness of behaviour cloning and one reason we continue with reinforcement learning.
