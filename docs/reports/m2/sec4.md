After the README setup, `python scripts/demo_network.py` prints both networks' sizes, runs the trained set network on 64 real observations (logits [64, 26], values [64]) and compares three of its decisions with Slack-aware's. A fresh set network then shows backpropagation of the policy and value losses and one Adam update (the loss falls); finally both trained networks dispatch a whole day.

The M1 GIF was too fast to follow, so `scripts/demo.py --policy set` now writes two videos: one shows eight consecutive decisions, each held before, during and after its action, with its orders highlighted and up to three of the network's most likely actions (Figure 2); the other plays the whole day at a labelled 5 simulated minutes per second. Ready-made videos are in `assets/`, new ones go to `outputs/`.
