After the README setup, `python scripts/demo_network.py` prints both networks' sizes, runs the trained set network on 64 real observations (logits [64, 26], values [64]) and compares three of its decisions with Slack-aware's. A fresh set network then shows backpropagation of the policy and value losses and one Adam update (the loss falls); finally both trained networks dispatch a whole day.

The M1 GIF was too fast to follow, so `scripts/demo.py --policy set` now writes the whole day as a replay web page: a single file that opens offline in any browser, where the reader plays, pauses, steps from decision to decision or drags a timeline. Each decision is shown in words with the situation it was taken in and up to three of the network's most likely actions (Figure 2). With `--video` it also saves a video that holds the 29 decisions of the afternoon peak one by one and a whole-day video at a smart speed that slows down for flights and fast-forwards charging and waiting. Ready-made replays and videos are in `assets/`.
