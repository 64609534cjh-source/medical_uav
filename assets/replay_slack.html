Medical UAV · one simulated day

Dispatcher

One drone picks up medical orders and delivers them before their deadlines. Each step below is one decision; the score is the share of orders delivered on time.

How to read this screen
  • Map: the square is the medical center M with charger C1, circles are hospitals, the bolt is charger C2, hatched areas are no-fly zones. Coloured squares next to a place are orders waiting there (all of them; the network sees at most 12); the three squares under the drone are its cargo slots.
  • Right: the current decision in words, the situation it was taken in and, for a network, up to three of its most likely actions (the drone takes the most likely one). Below: battery (the line marks the 15 % reserve), cargo and the most urgent waiting orders.
  • Timeline: grey bands are demand peaks, the green curve is the battery, ticks are decisions, dots are deliveries (green on time, amber late) and crosses are orders that expired. Click or drag it to jump.
  • At 1× flights play at 1 simulated minute per second, loading or unloading boxes at 1.5 and charging or waiting at 3, and each new decision holds the clock for about 2 s: its choices, the pick and its move into the action line. 2× and 3× play everything two or three times faster. The pauses are for reading, not the network's decision time: it decides in milliseconds. To stop at every decision, tick Stop at each decision.
  • On a small screen, zoom the browser out (Ctrl or Cmd and −) to see the whole page at once.
