Medical UAV · one simulated day

Dispatcher

One drone picks up medical orders and delivers them before their deadlines. Each step below is one decision; the score is the share of orders delivered on time.

How to read this screen
  • Map: the square is the medical center M with charger C1, circles are hospitals, the bolt is charger C2, hatched areas are no-fly zones. Coloured squares next to a place are orders waiting there; the three squares under the drone are its cargo slots.
  • Right: the current decision in words, the situation it was taken in and, for a network, its three most likely actions. Below: battery (the line marks the 15 % reserve), cargo and the most urgent waiting orders.
  • Timeline: grey bands are demand peaks, the green curve is the battery, ticks are decisions, dots are deliveries (green on time, amber late) and crosses are orders that expired. Click or drag it to jump.
  • Smart speed plays flights at 1 simulated minute per second and fast-forwards loading, charging and waiting at 8; it pauses briefly at each decision. The fixed speeds play everything at one rate.
